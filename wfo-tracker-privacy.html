WFO Tracker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: WFO Tracker doesn't have internet access, doesn't collect anything, and doesn't send your data anywhere - not to us, not to anyone. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What data the app stores

WFO Tracker stores the information you enter directly into the app - your daily work-location records, planned days, office locations, working-day schedule, weekly office target, and holiday list. All of it is stored locally on your device, in a private on-device database and app settings that only WFO Tracker itself can read.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect any data. WFO Tracker has no internet permission at all - it is technically incapable of sending anything to a server, because it doesn't have a server and can't reach the network.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs are included in the app.
  • We don't know who you are, how you use the app, or what you've logged. We have no way to find out.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, WFO Tracker schedules a local notification on your device to remind you to log your day. This happens entirely on-device through Android's own notification system - no data leaves your phone to make this work.

Android system backup

Like most Android apps, WFO Tracker allows Android's built-in Auto Backup feature. If you have this turned on for your device, Android may store an encrypted copy of the app's data in your own Google Account, the same way it backs up your other apps. This is a standard Android OS feature, tied to your Google account, and is not something Cabin Room Studio has access to or control over. You can turn this off in your device's system backup settings if you'd prefer not to use it.

Exporting and sharing your data

WFO Tracker lets you export your data as a backup file, and share filtered records via Android's share sheet. These actions are entirely under your control - the app only creates and shares a file when you explicitly choose to, and only to the destination you pick (e.g. saving to your device, or sharing through another app of your choosing).

Deleting your data

Uninstalling WFO Tracker removes all locally stored data (unless you've separately backed it up yourself via export, or via Android's system backup as described above). You can also delete individual records or wipe everything from within the app's Settings.

Children's privacy

WFO Tracker is a general-purpose work-attendance tool not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age - the app has no data-collection capability at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since the app fundamentally can't collect data without adding an entirely new capability (internet access) that doesn't currently exist, any material change here would be a significant, visible shift in what the app does - not a quiet update.
