OfficeTrack

Hybrid work, without the spreadsheet. OfficeTrack keeps track of where you work - office, home, leave, holiday or half day - and shows at a glance whether you are on track for your office target, week by week or month by month.

Everything stays on your phone

There is no account and no sign-up. Your days, targets and settings live on your device, and you can back them up, restore them or import a spreadsheet whenever you want. The free version shows ads, and the privacy policy sets out exactly what they use.
